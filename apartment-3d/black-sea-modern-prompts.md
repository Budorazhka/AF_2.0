# «Черноморский модерн» в 3D-прогулке

Материалы и картина сгенерированы встроенным ImageGen по мотивам ракурсов кухни и гостиной для планировки 40,5 м². Готовые текстуры WebP загружаются из `assets/residences/materials/black-sea-modern/`.

## Атлас материалов

Use case: product-mockup. Create ONE square production material atlas for the coastal Black Sea Modern interior of Aurum Fort. The entire square is four square orthographic close-up scans in a precise 2 by 2 grid with boundaries at exactly 50% width and height. No borders, labels or margins. TOP LEFT: clean warm medium honey oak millwork veneer, natural linear VERTICAL grain and very fine pores, lower contrast, matte oiled, no plank joints. TOP RIGHT: cream-beige Venetian lime plaster with a fine, visibly genuine hand-trowelled finish, broad soft mottling, no isolated big stains, matte. BOTTOM LEFT: honey-ivory vein-cut honed travertine stone with softly horizontal sand-colored bands, tiny filled pores and authentic light movement, warm, natural, restrained, no strong diagonal marble cracks. BOTTOM RIGHT: creamy oatmeal bouclé upholstery, fine irregular looped linen-wool fibers as small as a few millimetres, naturally pale neutral, tactile matte. Draw believable material scans that can be repeated and lit again in a 3D engine. Straight overhead orthographic camera, non-directional neutral illumination, absolute zero perspective, no glare, gradients, objects, letters, watermark, shadows or baked-in shading. Clean segment boundaries and full bleed. Keep these warm coastal tones light and delicate without a yellow cast.

## Картина

Use case: illustration-story. Create a single landscape 4:3 abstract coastal painting as a printable fine art texture for a premium Black Sea apartment. Minimal contemporary tonal impasto landscape of gentle Black Sea waves and a dim wooded headland near Batumi, warm chalk, desaturated sage grey, pale sand, an ivory sky. Eye-level view from shore, a low horizon at 57 percent of image height, broad softened brush marks, restrained quiet composition, shallow mineral texture, no obvious bright blue. Very soft coastal haze, expressive and authentic oil and plaster on canvas. Flat diffuse reproduction, the artwork fills the entire image edge to edge. No frame, mat, canvas edge, gallery, objects, people, boats, sun disc, signature, lettering, labels, watermarks or shadows.
