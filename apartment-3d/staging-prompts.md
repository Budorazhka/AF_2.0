# Материалы декора в 3D-прогулке

Атлас создан встроенным ImageGen для обоих вариантов ремонта. Четыре фактуры подготовлены как отдельные карты WebP в `assets/residences/materials/staging/`.

Use case: product-mockup. Make ONE square texture atlas for a furnished Black Sea coastal apartment in a 3D walkthrough. Four equally sized square orthographic macro material scans in a precise full-bleed 2x2 grid with seams exactly at 50 percent. TOP LEFT: buttery oatmeal knitted wool throw, natural fine cable stitches and small loops, warm cream, tightly focused perpendicular scan. TOP RIGHT: muted honey natural rattan basket weave with small flat diagonal interlaced strips, matte and no gaps, high craft detail. BOTTOM LEFT: warm ivory translucent-looking linen lamp shade material, tightly woven mild irregular fibres, very soft matte. BOTTOM RIGHT: matte warm mushroom taupe ceramic stoneware with subtle warm beige flecks and texture, nearly uniform. Each patch is a repeatable physically credible base-color photograph, perfectly flat, even neutral diffuse illumination, zero perspective, no shadows, gradients, gloss or baked illumination. No margins, gutters, borders, objects, labels or text.
